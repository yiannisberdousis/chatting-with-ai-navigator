# Chatting with AI — Full Pathway Part 1

## Part 1: Exploring Existing Chatbots

**Duration:** approximately 45 minutes  
**Pathway position:** Part 1 of 5  
**Student role:** critical evaluator  
**Optional GenAI role:** bias critic / reflection support

Part 1 is the entry point to the sequential Full Pathway:

**Part 1 → Part 2 → Part 3 → Part 4 → Part 5**

Students evaluate chatbot responses before designing chatbot logic in Part 2. They identify evidence, assumptions, possible bias, safety concerns, accuracy issues, and limitations, then complete one evidence-based **“A good chatbot should...”** principle. This principle and its evidence trace must be kept for Part 2, where it becomes a design requirement.

## Teacher Guide first

`ChattingwithAI_TeacherGuide_FULL_PART1_LITE_FINAL.docx` is the main implementation document. All other files support the Teacher Guide and should be selected only when needed.

The shorter Teacher Walkthrough is an optional in-class reference. It does not replace the Teacher Guide.

## 45-minute classroom logic

1. Choose **one route** only.
2. Evaluate **two chatbot responses**.
3. Select **one response for closer checking**.
4. Support the judgement with evidence.
5. Identify assumptions, bias, missing information, safety, accuracy, or limitations.
6. Complete one **Part 1 → Part 2 handover principle** with its evidence trace.

If time is tight, keep the second response evaluation brief and protect the closer check and final handover. Additional responses, GenAI-supported reflection, cross-route comparison, or response rewriting are optional extensions only.

## Minimum materials for one lesson

Use:

- the **Part 1 Teacher Guide** as the main implementation document;
- pages 1–4 of `03_PART1_Student_Worksheet.docx`;
- one route-specific file: Route A, Route B, or Route C.

Optional:

- `01_Teacher_Walkthrough_PART1_45min.docx` as an in-class quick reference;
- page 5 of the Student Worksheet only when GenAI-supported reflection is used;
- the Route Selection Sheet, Debrief Board, Observation Checklist, or GenAI prompts when they serve the selected implementation.

## Printing and distribution

- **Student Worksheet:** print pages 1–4 for every group. Print page 5 only when using the optional GenAI Reflection Trace.
- **Route A:** page 1 is the student prompt set; page 2 is teacher-only guidance.
- **Route B:** pages 1–3 contain student examples; page 4 is the teacher-only challenge map.
- **Route C:** page 1 contains setup instructions; give each group only its assigned bot-card page from pages 2–6. Page 7 is teacher-only guidance.
- **Observation Checklist:** one copy per group is sufficient when used.

## Part 1 → Part 2 continuity

Before the lesson ends, the teacher should collect, photograph, or digitally save each group’s final principle and evidence trace. At the beginning of Part 2, each group reuses this record as a design requirement.

No separate Part 1 portfolio or handover file is required. The Part 1 Student Worksheet is the canonical student record.

## Route summary

### Route A — Live Chatbot
Use when students may interact with a school-approved chatbot. Live outputs may vary. Use safe prompts and no personal information. Assign different themes across groups where possible.

### Route B — Provided Examples
Recommended default for first implementation, limited tool access, strict timing, or uncertain policy. The examples are intentionally designed to surface clarity, accuracy, assumptions, bias, safety, and limitations.

### Route C — Human-Bot Role-Play
Use for a no-tech implementation or to make simple rule-based chatbot logic visible. Use groups of at least three. Evaluate the rules and response pattern, not the student playing the bot.

## Core message

You do not need to use every file. Read the Teacher Guide, choose one route, evaluate two responses, and protect the final Part 1 → Part 2 handover.
