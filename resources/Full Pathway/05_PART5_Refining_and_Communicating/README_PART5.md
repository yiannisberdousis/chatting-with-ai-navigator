# Chatting with AI - Full Pathway Part 5

## Refining and Communicating

Part 5 closes the Full Pathway. Creator groups validate the Part 4 handover, confirm one evidence-based **Improve** or **Keep as designed** action, make no more than one bounded refinement where required, run one matched verification using the exact Part 4-reserved input, communicate the result and remaining limitations, and complete the pathway record.

## Start here

1. Read `ChattingwithAI_TeacherGuide_FULL_PART5_LITE_FINAL.docx`.
2. Confirm that every group has the tested prototype, the Part 3 Test Brief and the completed Part 4 Independent Test and Review Record.
3. Give each creator group one copy of `material_kit/student_common/01_PART5_Refinement_Verification_and_Completion_Record.docx`.
4. Use only the relevant route section in `material_kit/support/02_PART5_Route_Refinement_and_Save_Guide.docx`.
5. Use the optional teacher walkthrough for readiness, timing and quick-reference language.

## Timing

- **Standard route:** 60 minutes.
- **Compressed route:** 45 minutes only with a complete handover, pre-opened prototypes, one prepared route, no optional GenAI and no second revision cycle.

## Protected minimum

Every group must complete: a valid Part 5 entry gate; one evidence-linked Improve or Keep action; one matched verification using the reserved input; one Creator Statement with a remaining limitation or boundary; one final Part 1 principle judgement; and one saved final prototype state.

## Important boundaries

- A Part 4 `RETEST NEEDED` status must be resolved before Part 5 refinement begins.
- One main refinement only, or a justified Keep as designed decision.
- Do not change the chatbot purpose, target user or approved scope.
- GenAI may support implementation or communication only after students have reviewed the evidence and chosen the action themselves.
- Do not claim that the final chatbot is fully correct, safe, unbiased or validated with real users.

The Teacher Guide is authoritative. The material-kit files support specific steps and are not separate lesson plans.
